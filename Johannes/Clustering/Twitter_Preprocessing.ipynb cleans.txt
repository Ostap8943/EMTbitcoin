"Twitter_Preprocessing.ipynb" cleans data and processes URLs and emojis

"Bert_token_transformer.ipynb" prepares tweets for BERT by creating properly tokenized texts

"Twitter_Embedding.ipynb" creates tweet embeddings

"Embedding_Aggregator.ipynb" aggregates tweet embeddings to the user level (for training PCA/K-Means use cutoff 2021-10-19, for inference 2022-01-02)

"Pca_calculator.ipynb" calculates Incremental PCA

"cluster_finder.ipynb" tries different combinations of dimensions and K to find the best combination

"cluster_creator.ipynb" creates the final clustering

"cluster_labeling.ipynb" labels clusters based on meta-features and top-10 user tweets

"cluster_users.ipynb" assigns each user to a cluster
